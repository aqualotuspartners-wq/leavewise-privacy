Leavewise Privacy Policy

Aqua Lotus Partners · Effective November 24, 2026

Leavewise (“the app”) is a personal time-off tracker published by Aqua Lotus Partners. This policy explains what stays on your device and what advertising partners may collect when the app shows ads.

Leave records stay on your device

Balances, accrual rules, time-off entries, notes, company holidays, and reminder settings are stored locally in the app’s database on your phone. Leavewise does not create an account, does not ask you to sign in, and does not send those records to Aqua Lotus Partners or to an employer, payroll, or HR system. The app is not connected to your employer.

If you use Android backup or device transfer, Google’s backup of your phone may include the app’s local database and settings. That backup is controlled by your Google account settings, not by us.

You can delete the data by clearing the app’s storage or uninstalling Leavewise. Exporting a CSV shares a file only with the app you choose in the share sheet.

Advertising, consent, and the Advertising ID

Leavewise is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. Before ads are requested, the app uses Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request consent where required, such as in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom. Ads are not loaded until that consent check finishes. If you are offline, the app still works; ads simply may not appear.

When an ad is requested, Google may collect and use:

  • Advertising ID (a resettable device identifier from Android)
  • IP address, which can imply an approximate location
  • Device and app information, such as device model, language, and the ad SDK version
  • Ad interactions, such as whether an ad was shown or tapped
  • Diagnostic data related to the ads SDK

Google uses this information to show and measure ads, limit fraud, and remember the consent choice you made in the UMP form. Aqua Lotus Partners does not receive your leave records from AdMob and does not sell personal information. We do not add our own analytics or crash-reporting SDK.

You can reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in Android settings. Where the consent form requires it, Leavewise also offers an in-app control for ad privacy options. Google’s own policy describes AdMob and UMP in more detail: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state — used to request consent and load ads. Leave data is not uploaded.
  • Advertising ID — declared so AdMob can use the advertising identifier as described above.
  • Notifications — used only if you turn on reminders. The app asks for this permission at that moment, not at first launch. Reminders are scheduled on the device.
  • Run at startup — lets reminder scheduling continue after a reboot. Leavewise does not use exact alarms.

Children

Leavewise is a general-audience tool for tracking personal leave. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this page with a new effective date. Continued use of the app after the update means the new policy applies to the advertising practices it describes. Your leave records remain on your device either way.
